\section{Subsectors 03/CHEM}
\textbf{Purpose.} To describe, for each GSD of the group, the units funded, their money, their ERC sectors, institutions, career levels, gender and partners, using the GSD of the unit leaders.

\textbf{Result.} 81 funded units have a leader whose GSD starts with 03/CHEM (12 coordinators, 69 partners) in 36 projects and 13 ERC sectors; they received 15.83~M~EUR (6.09\% of the budget). The GSD is known for 509 of the 584 unit leaders of these 13 sectors (leaders of research bodies and of institutions that are not universities are not in the directory). The staff of the directory is known by CUN area, not by GSD, so nothing is divided by the staff of a GSD.
