\section{Scope}
The report covers 23 of the 28 ERC sectors of the PRIN 2026 call,
those for which the MUR had published the decree, the ranking (Tabella A) and the funded projects
(Tabella B) when the data were extracted: LS2, LS3, LS4, LS5, LS6, LS7, LS8, PE1, PE2, PE3, PE5, PE6, PE7, PE8, PE9, PE10, PE11, SH3, SH4, SH5, SH6, SH7 and SH8. Not yet published: PE4, LS1, LS9, SH1 and SH2.
Every figure and number refers to these 23 sectors only.

\subsection{Definitions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Budget}: the sum, over the 23 sectors, of the funds that the decrees make
available to each sector (under-40 pool plus ordinary pool): 208.29~M~EUR.
\item \textbf{Share}: MUR contribution received by an institution, region or other group, divided by the budget, in percent.
\item \textbf{PI} and \textbf{RU}: the coordinating unit of a project and its partner units; the money of a project is the sum of the MUR contributions of its units.
\item \textbf{Under-40 PI}: a principal investigator printed in green in the MUR tables.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Data}
180 funded projects and 900 funded units (180 PI, 720 RU) from Tabella B, receiving 195.96~M~EUR (94.08\% of the budget).
Tabella A lists 294 ranked projects, 180 of them funded; it contains the projects the evaluation committees judged fundable, not all the projects submitted.
Gender is inferred from the first given name with a table of 323 names (66 assigned by hand, 252 by the Italian ending rule); it is a binary inference used only in aggregate.
City, region and type of each institution come from a hand-checked table of 91 institutions.

\section{Analyses}
\subsection{Budget and its distribution}
\subsubsection{Budget and funded money per sector}
\textbf{Purpose.} To compare, sector by sector, the money funded with the budget the decrees make available, and to separate the money that went to projects whose PI is under 40.

\textbf{Result.} The budget is 208.29~M~EUR and 195.96~M~EUR (94.1\%) was funded. Per sector, the funded money is between 76.3\% of the budget of LS6 and 100.0\% of the budget of PE1. The unfunded part of the budget is 12.33~M~EUR; in 19 sectors it is smaller than 1.0~M~EUR, the smallest contribution received by a funded project, and in LS6 all the ranked projects are funded. 48 of the 180 funded projects have an under-40 PI; they received 52.82~M~EUR (27.0\% of the funded money). The under-40 pools of the 23 sectors add up to 30.98~M~EUR; in 16 sectors the money of the under-40 PIs' projects is larger than the sector's pool. 11 funded under-40 projects rank below the best unfunded project of an over-40 PI (LS4, LS5, LS7, LS8, PE1, PE5, PE8, SH5 and SH6).

\subsection{Institutions}
\subsubsection{Funding by institution}
\textbf{Purpose.} To show which institutions received the largest shares of the budget and how the budget is divided among institutions.

\textbf{Result.} 91 institutions have at least one funded unit. The largest share is that of CNR (7.0\%), followed by Sapienza (6.3\%) and Padova (4.4\%). \subsection{Geography}
\subsubsection{Money by region and city}
\textbf{Purpose.} To show where the money went: by region, by city, and, per region and macro-area, which part of the funding came from units coordinating projects and which from partner units.

\textbf{Result.} The regions with the largest shares are Lombardia (15.4\%), Lazio (13.3\%) and Campania (8.5\%). National research bodies (CNR, INAF, INFN, INGV), which have no region, received 8.8\%. No funded unit is in Valle d'Aosta. The 53 cities with a funded unit include Roma (11.9\%), Milano (10.7\%), Torino (6.6\%), Napoli (4.9\%) and Padova (4.4\%) as the five largest. The share of the money received by units coordinating projects is, by macro-area: North 31\%; Centre 20\%; South and Islands 25\%. Liguria, Molise, Basilicata and Calabria have no coordinating unit among their funded units. The table of regions is in Appendix C.
